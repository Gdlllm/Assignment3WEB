Formula 1 teams

In Formula 1 every team builds its own car. A team has two race drivers and hundreds of engineers, mechanics and strategists who work together to make the car fast and reliable.

What a team does

Before the season the team designs the car in its factory. During a race weekend the mechanics prepare the car, the engineers study the data and the strategists decide when the drivers should stop for new tyres.

Two championships

Each season has two titles. The Drivers' Championship is for the best driver, and the Constructors' Championship is for the best team. The points of both team drivers are added together for the team.

Teams of our drivers

Red Bull Racing

An Austrian team based in Milton Keynes, England. It joined Formula 1 in 2005 and won many titles with Sebastian Vettel and Max Verstappen.

Driver: Max Verstappen

Mercedes-AMG Petronas

A German team based in Brackley, England. It won eight Constructors' Championships in a row, from 2014 to 2021.

Driver: Kimi Antonelli

Scuderia Ferrari

The oldest and most famous team in Formula 1. Ferrari has raced in every World Championship season since 1950.

Legend: Michael Schumacher
